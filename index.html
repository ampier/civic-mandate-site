Civic Mandate

Lead a real democracy. Inherit its problems.

Civic Mandate is an offline strategy game about leading one of 33 real democracies. Set taxes and spending, pass policies, change real laws, appoint your cabinet, survive parliament and crises, and win the next election.

  • Free to start. A full first term in Estonia, Japan, or the United States, with nothing to buy.
  • Real laws. 546 laws across 33 countries, each starting where the country really stands today, with its source.
  • Plays offline. No account, and your campaign stays on your device.
  • No ads, no subscriptions. Optional one-time packs: Countries, Advanced policies, or the Complete edition with both.
  • Seven languages. English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.
  • Private by design. No analytics, no tracking, and nothing collected by the developer.
